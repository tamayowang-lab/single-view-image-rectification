RGB Active Observer V2: operational perception, actual-state 2.5D memory and an empirical pilot

The engineering is working; human likeness is not established. The full model remains behind the center-prior baseline on density and sequence measures. The sample is small and comes from an internal split of released training images. No parameter fitting or population significance claim is made.

What was actually implemented and tested

Pinned, SHA256-verified SSDLite320 MobileNetV3 and MiDaS Small run on CPU. Semantic detections are admitted only centrally from a foveated context crop; full-image semantic detections never become a hidden search bank. The depth map is private sensor output; central observed samples alone update memory. The same provider factory and caches are used by run-one, prepared previews and empirical evaluation.

Object memory now separates identity, image-position and depth uncertainty, observation count, exposure time, relative depth dispersion and relative 3D envelopes. Association rejects strong class conflicts and retains previously observed partial extents. Duration-dependent evidence is capped by a sensory ceiling. A computational expected-reduction proxy and bounded stay action are added, while legacy evidence and uncertainty×novelty remain selectable baselines. Stay segments are merged for fixation metrics.

Empirical protocol

  • Task: target-present categorical search from COCO-Search18, via a pinned research mirror.
  • Splits: 24 available originals sorted by a fixed filename hash: 12 train, 6 validation, 6 internal test images. This is not the official benchmark test split. No weights were fitted on these images.
  • Completed test: 6 images, 10 participants, 60 eligible trials, 3 stochastic seeds and 14 variants: 2,520 model runs. Seeds are averaged within image/participant before image-balanced summaries.
  • Geometry: reproduce the mirror's black-padding rule to the 1680×1050 display, then uniformly rescale pixels and coordinates to 640×400. Pixel hashes reject train/test duplication. The exact original stimulus pipeline may differ; confirmation needs official display assets. Foveation remains a declared pixel-based approximation.
  • Timing: the model's viewing budget is conditioned on each human trial RT; human cumulative dwell is truncated at RT. Saccade gaps are unavailable. This evaluates gaze under a supplied duration; it does not predict search response time.
  • Leakage: only the declared first fixation sets the initial gaze. No subsequent human coordinates, target boxes or dataset semantic annotations enter prediction.

Metric definitions and scope

NSS and heatmap correlation use generated duration-weighted densities. AUC uses deterministic nonfixated-pixel negatives and tie-correct SciPy ranks; it is not branded AUC-Judd. Sequence distance is our documented custom spatial DTW, not MultiMatch. Durations/amplitudes and refixations are available per trial/image/participant. The completed pilot reports per-trial mean timing/movement summaries; event-distribution Wasserstein functions are now implemented/tested in the evaluator but are not part of this completed pilot table. Adjacent stay segments are coalesced before scoring.

Limitations and next scientific priorities

  • Only six internal test images and ten participants. No reliable population inference or significance ranking is claimed.
  • The pretrained detector was trained on COCO and may have seen stimulus imagery. Internal image holdout is not a guarantee of vision-model pretraining independence.
  • Quality, covariance, expected information and durations are uncalibrated heuristics. Expected reduction can be optimistic for irreducible identity ambiguity.
  • Semantic category cues are central; task guidance lacks a learned goal-conditioned peripheral template. Context priors are empty unless explicitly configured.
  • Data association can still duplicate after severe crop changes or merge similar nearby regions. Envelopes are partial visible-surface uncertainty, never complete physical object shapes.
  • Actual inference is demonstrated on Linux CPU. Windows UTF-8 behavior is regression-tested, but native Windows pretrained inference is not claimed.
  • Next: calibrate on training images, audit display/retinal geometry, test parameter sensitivity, then evaluate on additional untouched images. Do not tune against this pilot and call the same images confirmatory.
